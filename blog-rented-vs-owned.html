Rented vs owned: what lead sites really cost a trade.

Lead and directory sites can fill a quiet week. But every enquiry is rented: you pay for it, often share it, and keep nothing when you stop. Here's how the two compare, and a simple sum to work out your own numbers.

How lead sites charge

Most lead and directory sites charge trades in one of two ways, and some use both:

  • A monthly membership to be listed, whether or not the work comes in.
  • A fee for each lead, often paid in credits. The price usually depends on your trade and the size of the job.

Either way, you pay for the chance to quote, not for the job. On many of these sites, the same job goes to several trades. So you're often quoting against others who paid for the same lead.

Work out what each job really costs

Take one month from your lead site's invoices and your own job list:

(membership + lead fees) ÷ jobs won = cost per job

Example, using made-up numbers: You spend £200 in a month and win 4 jobs. Each job cost you £50 before any materials. Win 2 jobs from the same spend and it's £100 each.

Do the same sum for your own website: the monthly fee divided by the jobs it brings in. The difference is that what you spend on your site keeps working for you after the month ends.

When a lead site still makes sense

They have their place. A lead site can help when:

  • you're just starting out and have no reviews yet
  • you're testing a new area before you commit to it
  • work is quiet and you need jobs this week

A new website takes time to climb Google, so it isn't an overnight switch. Many trades use a lead site as a top-up while their own site and reviews build up. Then they use it less as their own enquiries grow.

What owning it looks like

An owned site is set up to turn searches into calls you don't share:

  • A click-to-call button and a quote form that emails you straight away
  • Your Google Business Profile linked, so new reviews build up in one place
  • Search Console and Google Analytics set up from day one, so you can see how people find you
  • The code, the domain and every enquiry in your name. Cancel the care plan and you keep it all
