FAQs

Clear answers before you book.

These are the questions we hear most on our first call. We answer them here, so you know what to expect.

Q1Do I own my website, or do you license it back to me?+

You own your website and the code from day one — we do not lease it to you. The £29 a month plan pays for us looking after it, not for the right to keep it. Cancel it any time and your site keeps running.

Q2What happens if I cancel the care plan?+

Nothing goes away. Within 5 working days you get the handover pack: the site files, every login and a one-page guide. No penalty, no minimum time to stay.

Q3How fast is 5–7 days?+

This is our build time once we have your service list, job photos, and reviews. It starts once we have your details from the 30-minute details call, or by email. If something is missing, like a logo or photos, we start with a placeholder. We swap it in later. This can add a day or two.

Q4I don't have professional photos or reviews yet — can you still build it?+

Yes. Most trades start this way. Phone photos work fine to begin with. We build a review section too. It fills up as your Google reviews come in.

Q5Do you set up my Google Business Profile too?+

If you don't have one, we help you set it up and link it to your site. If you already have one, we connect it. Your reviews and map listing show up on your site.

Q6What if something needs changing after launch?+

You get one review round before launch, plus 30 days after launch to fix words, swap photos, and solve problems — all part of your £397.

Q7Is there a contract or minimum tie-in?+

No. The £397 is a one-time build fee. The £29 a month plan has no contract. Cancel it any time.

Q8I already have a website. Does a redesign cost more?+

No. A redesign is the same £397 and the same 5–7 days as a new site. We keep your domain and your Google reviews, and rebuild the pages around getting you calls: fast on a phone, a clear call button and quote form, and your reviews up front.

Q9What's the difference between the £397 and £897 packages?+

The £397 build is the website: 5 pages, live in 5–7 days. The £897 Brand + Website package adds a logo and brand kit, business email on your domain, up to 10 pages, a Google Business Profile tidy-up and print-ready artwork, and is live in 10–14 days. Both are one-off prices and you own everything.

Q10Can you build something bigger, like a CRM?+

Yes. Stock systems, lead inboxes, invoicing and CRMs are scoped to the job, like the Granite City Cars system. The database and every record sit in accounts in your name.
