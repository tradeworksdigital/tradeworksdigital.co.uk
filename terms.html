Terms of Business

The plain-English terms for our website builds and care plan: what you get, what you pay, and what happens if either of us wants to stop.

LAST UPDATED: 14 NOVEMBER 2026

Who we are

These terms are between you and Trade Works Digital Ltd (Tradeworks Digital), company no. 17391710, registered in England and Wales. Our registered office is at 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. Contact us at mosa@tradeworksdigital.co.uk.

They apply when you confirm a build with us in writing (email is fine) and pay your deposit.

What you get

  • Website build, £397: a new website or a redesign of your current one, as described in the offer on the day you confirm.
  • Brand + Website, £897: everything in the website build, plus the brand and extra items listed in the offer on the day you confirm.
  • Care plan, £29 a month: optional. Hosting, security, backups, domain renewals and small changes, as described in the offer.
  • Custom work: anything else is agreed in a written proposal with its own price and timeline.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are fixed. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added: the price on the offer is the price you pay.
  • You pay 50% as a deposit to hold your build slot. The other 50% is due when you approve the site, before it goes live.
  • Build payments are by bank transfer, to the details on your invoice.
  • The care plan is paid monthly in advance, by card or Direct Debit.
  • Email providers charge for mailboxes. If your package includes business email, we set it up and the provider bills you directly.

Timing

  • The website build goes live within 7 days of us receiving your photos and service list. If we miss that, your first 3 months of the care plan are free.
  • Brand + Website takes 10–14 days from receiving your details, because brand work needs a second look.
  • The clock pauses while we wait for content, answers or approval from you.
  • If we don't hear from you for 14 days, the build is paused. After 60 days without a reply the build is closed, and payments made are kept. Starting again needs a new booking.

Changes

  • The website build includes one round of changes before launch. Brand + Website includes two.
  • Both include 30 days of fixes after launch: wording, photo swaps and anything that isn't working.
  • Work beyond what your package lists gets a clear quote and timeline first. Nothing extra is charged without your agreement.

Cancelling a build

  • Cancel before we start work and your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Once work has started, the deposit is not refunded, and nothing further is owed.
  • If we can't complete your build, we refund everything you've paid.

Cancelling the care plan

  • There is no contract and no minimum term. Cancel any month and it stops at the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Within 5 working days you get the handover pack: the site files, every login and a one-page guide.
  • If you cancel, hosting and domain renewals become yours to arrange. We tell you exactly what to do.

What you own

  • Once the build is paid in full, you own the website, its code, the words, the logo and brand files we made for you, and your photos.
  • Your domain is registered in your name from day one. Your Google Business Profile, Search Console and Google Analytics are set up with you as owner.
  • Some building blocks are licensed from others, such as fonts and open-source code. You get the right to keep using them on your site.
  • We only show your site in our portfolio or case studies if you say yes. We'll ask you first.

What we need from you

  • Your service list, area, photos and any reviews you want shown, and replies to questions during the build.
  • Confirmation that you have the right to use the photos, logos and wording you send us.
  • Accurate business details. You are responsible for the claims your site makes about your work, prices and qualifications.

What we can't promise

  • We build sites to be found and to turn visitors into enquiries, but nobody can guarantee a position on Google or a number of calls.
  • Outside services such as Google, your email provider and hosting companies can change or have faults that we don't control.

If something goes wrong

Tell us and we will put it right. Our total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the problem. We are not liable for lost profit or lost business. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit, or takes away rights you have by law.

Changes to these terms
