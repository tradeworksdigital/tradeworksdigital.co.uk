Build diary · Car dealer · Aberdeen · November 2026

Moving a car dealer off Car Dealer 5, onto a system they own.

The full story behind the Granite City Cars build: what we found on the old site, every screen before and after, how long it takes a buyer to get in touch, how we moved everything across, and the admin GCC run from a phone.

What we found

Before building anything, we checked the old site.

Every point here is dated and measured, so you can see exactly what changed.

SPEED · 10 NOV 2026

Slow on a phone

Google's PageSpeed test on a phone scored the homepage 48 out of 100. Its main content took 9.9 seconds to appear, and the page jumped about as it loaded (layout shift 0.834; Google counts anything over 0.25 as poor).

SEARCH · 6 OCT 2026

The stock page pointed Google at Nottingham

The homepage's “View Stock” link went to /used/cars/nottingham/. The page said Aberdeen, but its code gave Google the Nottingham address as its official one. It has since been changed to an Aberdeen address.

GOOGLE · SEARCH CONSOLE

No sitemap given to Google

Google had never been sent a list of the site's pages. The new site's sitemap, with every car on it, is submitted in Search Console at launch.

CONTACT · CAR PAGES

No WhatsApp, and the buttons were below the photos

Car pages offered Enquire Now, Request Callback and Test Drive further down the page. Each opened a form asking for a name, phone number and email.

The system behind it

Run from a phone.

GCC manage their stock, enquiries and invoices from a phone. Nothing goes through a platform.

Customer names, phone numbers, sale prices and totals are hidden. The enquiries shown are our own pre-launch test enquiries.

The move

Off the old platform, without losing anything.

The riskiest part of the job wasn't the new site. It was getting everything out of the old one before it was switched off.

  • Photos first. Every car photo was downloaded from the old platform while it was still live, compressed and re-uploaded in the same order, with the originals kept as a backup.
  • Stock, sales, invoices and enquiries were exported and imported, so the new system started with real history. Invoice numbers carry on from the old system.
  • Every old address still works. All 87 old addresses (car pages, stock searches, forms, reviews) go to the right new page, so links in Google, old adverts and customers' messages don't break.
  • A final export on switch day picked up the last enquiries and a sale that came in during the build.
